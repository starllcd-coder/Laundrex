Laundrex Usage Guide (English)

Overview
- Laundrex is a laundry operations system for POS, order tracking, pickup, delivery, payments, reports, and management.
- Most pages are available from the right-side menu. You must be logged in to access them.

Getting Started
1) Open the app URL in your browser.
2) Log in with your account.
3) Select your preferred language (EN/AR) from the top bar.
4) Select your branch from the top bar (if available).

Dashboard
- Shows quick KPIs for today and recent activity.
- Use it as a starting point to jump to Orders, POS, Payments, or Reports.

POS (Point of Sale)
1) Go to POS -> New Order.
2) Add items and services.
3) Confirm quantities, prices, and discounts.
4) Save the order or Save & Print.
5) A receipt can be printed from the order detail page.

Orders List
- Use filters to search by order number, phone, status, branch, date, or flags.
- Click an order number to open order details.
- Use Next/Open actions to move an order forward in the workflow.

Orders Kanban
- Drag-free columns show orders by status.
- Use quick actions per order (if enabled) to advance status.

Order Detail
- Shows customer, totals, items, and history.
- Use Mark Urgent / Clear Urgent as needed.
- Use Reprint Receipt to print again.
- Use Move to: <status> to advance the order.

Pickup Counter
1) Go to Orders -> Pickup Counter.
2) Scan QR or enter order number.
3) Confirm details, then Mark Ready or Mark Delivered.
4) Reprint receipt if needed.

Delivery
- Delivery List shows delivery orders and their status.
- Delivery Detail lets you set driver, address, fee, and time slot.
- Save Delivery to update the order delivery info.

Payments
- Payments list shows all recorded payments.
- From an order detail, you can Collect Payment and add references.

Customers
- Customers list shows registered customers and VIP status.
- Customer detail allows editing profile, VIP, language, WhatsApp opt-in, and notes.
- Order history shows the latest orders for the customer.

Reports
- Z Report shows daily summary and can be printed.
- Use filters for date ranges or branches if available.

Management
- Manage branches, staff, and audit logs (if permissions allow).
- Use roles to control access (Admin, Manager, Cashier, Worker).

Settings
- Configure catalog, taxes, and system preferences.
- Configure WhatsApp templates and messaging options.

Common Workflows
- New order: POS -> Add items -> Save & Print -> Track in Orders.
- Pickup: Pickup Counter -> Lookup -> Mark Ready/Delivered.
- Delivery: Delivery List -> Open -> Assign driver -> Save Delivery.

Troubleshooting
- If you cannot see a page, verify your permissions.
- If Arabic labels do not appear, refresh and ensure the AR language is selected.
- If a receipt does not open, allow popups in your browser.

Support
- Contact your system administrator for account or permission issues.
